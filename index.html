Welcome to Ingomso Lethu

Ingomso Lethu Wellness & Rehabilitation Centre is presented as a Christian-centred rehabilitation and wellness facility. Our message is simple: creating a better future through Christ.

Wellness

A structured environment focused on supporting residents during their stay at the centre.

Rehabilitation

The centre provides a residential rehabilitation setting with rules and procedures for residents and their families.

Faith

The centre operates in a church setting and asks parents, guardians and visitors to respect the applicable church rules.

Centre Information

Visiting Hours

Monday to Saturday, from 08:00 to 17:00, according to the centre contract. Parents are advised to make contact by telephone or WhatsApp before visiting.

Length of Stay

The contract states that the duration of stay at the facility is six months, with renewal arrangements applying if the stay exceeds that period.

Family & Support

Parents, sponsors and guardians are asked to work with the centre, follow its regulations and respect the owner, workers and church premises.

Please read the official contract. The downloadable PDF below contains the centre's admission, payment, visiting, conduct, release and declaration requirements. The PDF is provided from the contract supplied by the centre.
